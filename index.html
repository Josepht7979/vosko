The X300 pump won't prime after install — how do I fix it?
Let's get it primed. Try these in order:
  1. Close the discharge valve, open the suction valve fully.
  2. Fill the pump casing through the priming port until it overflows.
  3. Run for 30s — if no flow, check the suction line for air leaks.
